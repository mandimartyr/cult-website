# SYMPTOMS ARE NOT THE PROBLEM.

LEADS ARE FLAT.

EMAIL IS QUIET.

THE SITE FEELS OLD.

SEO ISN’T MOVING.

PAID IS GETTING EXPENSIVE.

CULT. looks across the system to understand:

what matters commercially

what should be fixed first

what should stop

what should be tested

what should wait

The Attention Audit turns scattered symptoms into a sequence.
