## How clients buy (public model — keep this)

Three doors. Everything else is capability detail under them.

| Door | Public name (live site) | When | Public floor (CAD) |
|------|-------------------------|------|--------------------|
| **01 Find it** | The Attention Audit | Constraint unclear | From **$1,500** |
| **02 Build it** | The Growth Build | Problem defined; need it made | From **$4,000** |
| **03 Run it** | CULT. Partner | Ongoing strategy + execution | From **$4,000 / month** |

**Rules**
- Ad spend billed separately, paid by client.
- Setup fees for ads, email, CRM, analytics, SEO — never bury in monthly only.
- Two revision rounds included; extra billed.
- Rush (<7 business days): +25–50%.
- Never say “we do everything.” Say the promise above, then price by outcome.
