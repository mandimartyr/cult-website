# Generated NOCT. direction

Built-in image generation was used for six design concepts and eight production photographs. Originals are retained at the paths in `asset-sources.json`; responsive production WebPs are committed under `public/studies/noct/`. User approved the new concept images. All production images were opened and visually inspected, including every NOCT. product label.

## Shared production prompt

Photorealistic high-fashion fragrance campaign asset for fictional brand exactly NOCT. Never write NOCTURNE or another brand. Severe rectangular black glass 50 ML bottle, straight rectangular black cap, matte black front label, white bold grotesk sans NOCT. and tiny EAU DE PARFUM / 50 ML / BATCH 0213. Consistent industrial glass object; no ornate cap, gold or serif. Nearblack, bone, dirty silver, restrained deep wine taillights. Raw photographic hard flash, grain and real texture. Standalone photography; no website UI, borders, captions or extraneous text.
