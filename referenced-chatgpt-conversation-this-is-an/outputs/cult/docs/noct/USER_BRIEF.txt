# CENTRAL CREATIVE IDEA

NOCT. is not positioned as perfume for everyone.

It is a fragrance built around the psychological shift that occurs after dark.

Night changes behaviour.

People dress differently.
Move differently.
Desire differently.
Become less explainable.

The fragrance should feel connected to:

* anticipation
* anonymity
* skin
* heat
* distance
* memory
* nightlife
* hotel corridors
* taxis
* wet pavement
* lipstick on glass
* empty streets
* 2:13 AM
* someone leaving
* someone arriving
* the smell of another person on clothing the next morning

The brand should sell a feeling of **after-hours identity**, not ingredients alone.

A useful conceptual line:

**THE NIGHT HAS A SCENT.**

Other campaign language can emerge from that thought.

Potential lines:

**FRAGRANCE FOR AFTER DARK.**

**WEAR IT AFTER THE LIGHT CHANGES.**

**NOT EVERYTHING SHOULD FOLLOW YOU HOME.**

**2:13 AM. STILL ON YOUR SKIN.**

**FOR THE HOURS YOU DON'T EXPLAIN.**

**WHAT HAPPENS AFTER DARK STAYS ON SKIN.**

Do not use all of these.

Curate them.

Copy must remain restrained.

# INTERACTION DIRECTION

Give this page more unique interaction than HARDLINE.

Potential interactions:

* cursor subtly reveals portions of photographs from darkness
* timestamps increment as sections change
* bottle rotates minimally with scroll
* typography slips fractionally out of alignment during transitions
* tiny points of light / flash exposure
* image sequences advancing with scroll
* campaign frames arriving like fragments of memory

* scent notes changing with time

Keep interaction restrained.
