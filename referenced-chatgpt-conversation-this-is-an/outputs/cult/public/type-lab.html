THE HIERARCHY.

Recommendation: use direction 01 for CULT. If long-form reading becomes a bigger part of the site, direction 02 keeps Space Grotesk strictly in the reading role.

  • One display family for all shared CULT heroes and section headings.
  • Mono for navigation, labels, buttons, numbers and supporting copy.
  • Scale and spacing create rank. Adding another heavy face does not.
  • Keep body tracking natural. Add tracking only to small uppercase labels.
  • Let each case study own its brand fonts inside its project; keep the surrounding CULT navigation consistent.
