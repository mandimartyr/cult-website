01 / The category
Seductive.
Mysterious.
Timeless.
Irresistible.
Every fragrance says the same thing.
Luxury fragrance is saturated with abstraction: desire, mystery, seduction, confidence. The language changes. The promise rarely does. NOCT. needed a more specific territory. Not another fragrance about attraction. A fragrance about when attraction changes.
02 / The position
NOCT. doesn’t sell a scent.
It sells the hour after the rules change.
Daytime identity is public. Nighttime identity is chosen. NOCT. occupies that transition — anticipation, anonymity, heat, the smell of another person on clothing the next morning.
