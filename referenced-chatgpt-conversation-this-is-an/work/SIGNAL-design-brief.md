# SIGNAL — simplified design brief

Explain why signals matter: a meaningful change helps someone spot a problem or opportunity and decide what to do next. Define this before presenting examples.

Keep Block 02 and the interactive experiences. Make each interaction easy to understand, and let visitors control the final reveal. Retain the response selector and optional local question preview with plain labels.

Use a short narrative: what changed → why it matters → what to do next. Remove repetition, unexplained acronyms, arbitrary dashboard counts, and technical catalogues. Name the sample report Your daily update and explain what each item means.

Keep SIGNAL's red, black and paper identity and useful campaign artwork. Clearly identify this as a self-initiated concept with illustrative data. Preserve the approved CULT Services hero.
